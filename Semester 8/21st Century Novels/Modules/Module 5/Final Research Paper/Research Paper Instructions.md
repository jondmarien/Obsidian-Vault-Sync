# Research Paper Instructions

**Introduction:**

This final assignment represents an opportunity for you to expand on the literary concepts that you have been exposed to in this course. Further, it will allow you to analyze, comparatively and in depth, two of the novels from our syllabus.

**Requirements:**

****Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools such as ChatGPT and Copilot should NOT be used for any part of this assignment.**** 

Choose two novels from our syllabus and write a comparative paper that addresses one or more central themes present in both novels. Your paper should have introductory and concluding paragraphs, a clear argumentative thesis, at least four paragraphs of development, and proper MLA in-text citation and a Works Cited page. (See the Orientation Module for MLA guidelines.) As this is a research paper, your work should be supported not only with relevant textual details from both novels but also with a minimum of four secondary sources, two of which may be scholarly articles from the lesson content. Please use the Sheridan Library Database—not Google—for your research. Your paper should be between 2,000 and 2,500 words. Be sure to consult the rubric for this assignment prior to submitting to the Dropbox.

**Suggested areas of focus, about the twenty-first century novel:**
- Trauma
- Feminism 
- Family relationships 
- Environmental anxieties 
- Preoccupations of the twenty-first century novel
- Social change and social justice

**--------------------**

**Length:** Between 2,000 and 2,500 words
**Format:** MLA

You are welcome to contact the professor to discuss your ideas for the Research paper including your thesis statement and the structure and organization of your assignment. The professor and Sheridan librarians can also answer questions you have about the writing process.
