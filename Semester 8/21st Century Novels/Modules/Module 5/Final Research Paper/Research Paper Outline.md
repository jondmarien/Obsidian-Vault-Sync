# Research Paper Outline

## **Comparative Analysis of Trauma in _Extremely Loud & Incredibly Close_ and _The Namesake_**

**I. Introduction (300-400 words)**
- Hook: Begin with a broad statement on trauma in 21st-century novels, referencing suggested foci like preoccupations with loss and healing.
- Brief overview of novels: Summarize EL&IC's post-9/11 grief and _The Namesake_'s immigrant dislocation.
- Thesis Statement: "In Jonathan Safran Foer's _Extremely Loud & Incredibly Close_ and Jhumpa Lahiri's _The Namesake_, trauma manifests as a disruptive force that propels protagonists on quests for identity and connection, ultimately revealing how personal and cultural wounds in the twenty-first century foster resilience through vulnerable communication and intergenerational reconciliation."
- Roadmap: Outline the paper's structure, signaling comparative analysis.

**II. Body Paragraph 1: Individual Manifestations of Trauma (400-500 words)**
- Topic Sentence: Explore how trauma isolates protagonists internally.
- EL&IC: Analyze Oskar's guilt and inventions as coping mechanisms (tie to your sample's phone call secret and scrapbook).
- _The Namesake_: Contrast with Gogol's name-induced identity crisis and rebellion against heritage.
- Comparison: Discuss parallels in external quests (Oskar's key search vs. Gogol's name change) using textual quotes and 1-2 secondary sources (e.g., on psychological trauma).
- Transition to intergenerational aspects.

**III. Body Paragraph 2: Intergenerational Transmission of Trauma (400-500 words)**
- Topic Sentence: Examine how trauma ripples through families.
- EL&IC: Detail grandfather's silence from Dresden and its impact on Oskar (expand your sample's mutism analysis).
- _The Namesake_: Parallel with Ashoke and Ashima's unspoken immigrant pains affecting Gogol.
- Comparison: Argue shared themes of inherited silence vs. voiced rebellion, with scholarly support (e.g., articles on diaspora trauma).

**IV. Body Paragraph 3: Narrative Techniques in Expressing Trauma (400-500 words)**
- Topic Sentence: Compare how authors use form to convey trauma's inexpressibility.
- EL&IC: Highlight visual elements and non-linear structure (from your sample's flip-book and photos).
- _The Namesake_: Contrast with introspective, linear narrative revealing subtle emotional layers.
- Comparison: Link to 21st-century preoccupations, using sources on postmodern vs. realist styles.

**V. Body Paragraph 4: Paths to Healing and Resilience (400-500 words)**
- Topic Sentence: Analyze resolutions through vulnerability and connection.
- EL&IC: Discuss confessions and cathartic acts (e.g., digging up the coffin from your sample).
- _The Namesake_: Parallel with Gogol's acceptance of heritage post-loss.
- Comparison: Synthesize how both novels advocate sharing stories for healing, tying back to thesis.

**VI. Conclusion (300-400 words)**
- Restate thesis and key insights.
- Broader implications: Connect to 21st-century themes like social change.
- End with a forward-looking statement on trauma's role in literature.

**VII. Works Cited**
- List primary texts (Foer and Lahiri novels).
- Minimum 4 secondary sources: Two from lesson content, others from Sheridan Database (e.g., JSTOR articles on trauma in each novel).
